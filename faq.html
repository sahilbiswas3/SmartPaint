Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common queries about our interior services.

Got Questions? We've Got Answers.

Here are some of the most commonly asked questions by our customers. If you don't see your question here, feel free to contact us.

How do I book a service?

Just give us a call or fill out our contact form. We'll schedule a free site visit at your convenience.

Is the site visit really free?

Yes, absolutely. Our initial site visit is 100% free with no hidden charges.

Do you provide materials like paint or marble, or do I need to buy them?

We can arrange all materials like Berger paints, tiles, and wood. Or, you can purchase them yourself — your choice.

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on the size. A single room painting job can be done in 1–2 days, while a full home interior may take 1–2 weeks.

Which paint brand do you use?

We commonly use Berger, but we can work with Asian Paints or other brands if you prefer.

What if I'm not happy with something after the work is done?

Your satisfaction is our priority. Let us know, and we'll fix it as soon as possible.

Do you offer warranty or after-service support?

Yes. Some paint and materials come with a manufacturer warranty, and we're always here if you need post-project help.

Can I see examples of your previous work?

Absolutely. Visit our Gallery Page to see real photos from our projects.
